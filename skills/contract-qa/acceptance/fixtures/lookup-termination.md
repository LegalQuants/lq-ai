# PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Professional Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into
as of February 10, 2026 between **Harborlight Analytics Ltd.**, a
Delaware corporation ("Client"), and **Foxglove Marketing LLC**, a New
York limited liability company ("Consultant").

**1. Services.** Consultant will provide the marketing analytics
services described in one or more statements of work ("SOWs") executed
by both parties.

**2. Fees.** Client will pay the fees stated in each SOW, invoiced
monthly in arrears, net thirty (30) days. Expenses require Client's
prior written approval and are reimbursed at cost.

**3. Term.** This Agreement begins on the date above and continues for
two (2) years unless terminated earlier under Section 4.

**4. Termination.**
(a) *For convenience.* Client may terminate this Agreement or any SOW
for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to
Consultant. Consultant may terminate this Agreement for convenience
upon ninety (90) days' prior written notice, provided no SOW is then in
progress.
(b) *For cause.* Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other
party materially breaches and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days of
written notice.
(c) *Effect.* Upon termination, Client will pay for services performed
and approved expenses incurred through the effective date of
termination.

**5. Intellectual Property.** Deliverables specified in an SOW are
assigned to Client upon full payment. Consultant retains its
pre-existing tools and methodologies, which are licensed to Client as
needed to use the deliverables.

**6. Confidentiality.** Each party will protect the other's non-public
information with reasonable care and use it only under this Agreement,
for three (3) years after disclosure.

**7. Limitation of Liability.** Neither party is liable for indirect or
consequential damages. Each party's aggregate liability is capped at
the fees paid or payable under the SOW giving rise to the claim in the
twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

**8. Independent Contractor.** Consultant is an independent contractor.
Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, or joint
venture relationship.

**9. General.** Governed by New York law; venue New York County.
Neither party may assign without consent, except to a merger successor.
Entire agreement; written amendments only.
