# NON-DISCLOSURE AND EVALUATION AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure and Evaluation Agreement (this "Agreement") is
entered into as of May 21, 2026 by and between **Juniper Grid Systems
Inc.**, a Nevada corporation with offices at 15 Illustrative Parkway,
Reno, Nevada ("Discloser"), and **Bluewater Logistics Corp.**, a
Delaware corporation with offices at 310 Notional Street, Portland,
Oregon ("Recipient").

**1. Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all
information disclosed by Discloser relating to its grid-management
technology, in any form, whether or not marked, including product
designs, specifications, pricing, and business plans.

**2. Use Restriction.** Recipient shall use Confidential Information
solely to evaluate a potential purchase of Discloser's products and for
no other purpose.

**3. Survival of Obligations.** Recipient's obligations under this
Agreement survive for seven (7) years following the later of the date of
last disclosure or termination of this Agreement, and in perpetuity for
any information constituting a trade secret.

**4. Non-Competition.** During the term of this Agreement and for
eighteen (18) months thereafter, Recipient shall not develop, market, or
sell any product or service that competes with Discloser's
grid-management technology in any market in which Discloser operates or
plans to operate.

**5. Feedback; Assignment.** Recipient hereby irrevocably assigns to
Discloser all right, title, and interest in and to any feedback,
suggestions, evaluation results, or improvement ideas provided by
Recipient relating to Discloser's technology, including all intellectual
property rights therein, without compensation.

**6. Indemnification.** Recipient shall indemnify, defend, and hold
harmless Discloser and its officers, directors, and employees from and
against all claims, losses, damages, and expenses (including reasonable
attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to any breach of this
Agreement by Recipient or its representatives.

**7. Standard Obligations.** Recipient shall protect Confidential
Information with reasonable care, may share it only with employees who
need to know it for the evaluation and who are bound by written
confidentiality obligations, and shall promptly notify Discloser of any
unauthorized disclosure.

**8. Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information
that is publicly available through no fault of Recipient, previously
known to Recipient, received from a third party without restriction, or
independently developed without use of Confidential Information.

**9. Return.** Upon written request, Recipient shall return or destroy
all Confidential Information within ten (10) business days.

**10. Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the
State of Nevada. Exclusive venue lies in the courts located in Washoe
County, Nevada.

**11. Entire Agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement of the
parties concerning its subject matter and may be amended only in
writing signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties execute this Agreement as of the date
first written above.
