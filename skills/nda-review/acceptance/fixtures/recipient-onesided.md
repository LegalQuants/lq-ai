This Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is
made as of April 14, 2026 by and between **Cobalt Peak Software Inc.**,

Confidential Information.

**1. Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means any
and all information disclosed by or on behalf of Company to Recipient,

confidential, including without limitation all business, technical,
financial, product, pricing, customer, and personnel information, all
notes, analyses, and derivatives thereof, and the existence and terms of
the parties' discussions.

Confidential Information solely to evaluate Company's platform; (c) not

without Company's prior written consent in each instance.

**3. No Exclusions for Compelled Disclosure.** If Recipient is required
by law to disclose Confidential Information, Recipient shall first
obtain Company's written consent and shall resist disclosure to the
maximum extent permitted.

(2) years thereafter, Recipient shall not, directly or indirectly,

actual breach will cause Company irreparable harm and consents to entry

**10. Miscellaneous.** This Agreement is the entire agreement of the
parties regarding its subject matter. Company may assign this Agreement
freely; Recipient may not assign it. No amendment is effective unless
