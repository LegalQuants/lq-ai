# Fictional vendor agreement — demo packet v1

This invented agreement is a software demonstration fixture. No party, service
or obligation described here is real. Effective date: 1 January 2026.

## §1 Parties and service
Paper Lantern Ltd (Customer) subscribes to the team scheduling service supplied
by Cedar Cloud Ltd (Supplier) for up to 20 users.

## §2 Payment
Customer shall pay USD 200 per month, invoiced monthly in advance. Undisputed
invoices are payable within 30 days of receipt. Customer must notify Supplier
of a disputed amount within 15 days of receipt and pay undisputed amounts on
time. Supplier may suspend service for an undisputed invoice remaining unpaid
15 days after Supplier gives written notice. This agreement specifies no late
fee and no procedure for resolving a disputed amount.

## §3 Implementation
Supplier will provide two remote onboarding sessions within 20 business days
after Customer supplies the user list. Customer is responsible for validating
imported data. No migration service, implementation milestone payment, or
completion acceptance procedure is specified.

## §4 Support
Supplier will acknowledge support requests within two business days. Support
hours are 09:00–17:00 Singapore time on Singapore business days. This agreement
does not specify a resolution deadline, uptime commitment or service credits.

## §5 Term and renewal
The initial term is 12 months from the Effective Date. The agreement renews for
successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice of
non-renewal at least 30 days before the current term ends. No limit on the number
of renewals is specified.

## §6 Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach remains uncured
30 days after written notice describing the breach. Customer may terminate for
convenience on 60 days' written notice but remains liable for subscription fees
through the end of the current term. Supplier will provide a CSV export of
Customer data within 10 business days after termination upon Customer's request.
The agreement does not specify how long that export request right remains open.

## §7 Surviving obligations
Accrued payment obligations and the confidentiality obligations in §8 survive
termination. There is no survival period stated for confidentiality.

## §8 Confidentiality
Each party will use the other's confidential information only to perform this
agreement and will disclose it only to personnel who need to know it for that
purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations.
