# MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Master Supply Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of
October 6, 2026 between **Foxglove Retail Group Inc.**, a Delaware
corporation ("Buyer"), and **Pinwheel Accessories LLC**, an Ohio
limited liability company ("Supplier").

**1. Supply; Forecasts.** Supplier will supply the products listed in
Exhibit 1 as ordered by Buyer. Forecasts provided by Buyer are
non-binding, but Supplier must maintain capacity and inventory
sufficient to fill any order up to 150% of the most recent forecast.

**2. Pricing; Most Favored Nation.** Prices are set in Exhibit 1 and
may be reduced by Buyer on thirty (30) days' notice to match market
conditions. Supplier warrants that the prices charged to Buyer are and
will remain no less favorable than those charged to any other customer
for similar quantities; if Supplier offers a lower price to any other
customer, that price applies retroactively to all outstanding and
future orders from Buyer.

**3. Payment; Chargebacks.** Payment is net one hundred twenty (120)
days. Buyer may deduct from any payment: chargebacks for late delivery
(2% of order value per day, uncapped), shortage claims, promotional
allowances, and handling fees for nonconforming packaging.

**4. Delivery.** Delivery is DDP (Incoterms 2020) at each Buyer
distribution center. Title and risk of loss pass on Buyer's acceptance.
Buyer may reject any shipment in whole or in part, at any time within
one hundred eighty (180) days of receipt, for any nonconformity.

**5. Warranty.** Supplier warrants the products for five (5) years from
the date of retail sale, including against defects arising from
ordinary wear. Supplier will accept return of all warranty-claimed
units at full invoice price plus a 15% handling fee, without
verification of the claimed defect.

**6. Indemnification.** Supplier shall indemnify, defend, and hold
harmless Buyer, its affiliates, and their officers, directors, and
employees from and against any and all claims, losses, damages,
penalties, and expenses of any kind arising out of or relating in any
way to the products or this Agreement, including claims arising from
Buyer's own negligence.

**7. Limitation of Liability.** BUYER'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS
AGREEMENT IS CAPPED AT THE AMOUNTS PAYABLE FOR THE SPECIFIC ORDER
GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM. SUPPLIER'S LIABILITY IS NOT CAPPED. NEITHER
PARTY'S EXCLUSION OF INDIRECT DAMAGES APPLIES TO SUPPLIER'S INDEMNITY
OBLIGATIONS.

**8. Termination.** Buyer may terminate this Agreement or any order at
any time for convenience without compensation for work in progress or
raw-material commitments. Supplier may terminate only for Buyer's
failure to pay undisputed amounts, uncured for one hundred eighty (180)
days.

**9. Audit.** Buyer may audit Supplier's records, facilities, and
sub-suppliers on twenty-four (24) hours' notice at Supplier's expense.

**10. Compliance; Insurance.** Supplier will comply with Buyer's
supplier code of conduct as amended by Buyer from time to time and
maintain product-liability insurance of at least $10,000,000 per
occurrence naming Buyer as additional insured.

**11. General.** Governed by the laws of the State of Washington; venue
King County. Buyer may assign freely; Supplier may not assign without
consent. Entire agreement; Buyer may amend its purchasing policies
referenced herein at any time.
