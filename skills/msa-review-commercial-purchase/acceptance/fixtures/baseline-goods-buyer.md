# MASTER PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Master Purchase Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of
September 2, 2026 between **Juniper Grid Systems Inc.**, a Nevada
corporation ("Buyer"), and **Bluewater Components Corp.**, a Delaware
corporation ("Supplier").

**1. Scope; Orders.** Supplier will sell and Buyer will purchase the
industrial vibration sensors and related calibration services described
in purchase orders issued under this Agreement. Each accepted purchase
order incorporates this Agreement. In case of conflict, this Agreement
controls unless the purchase order expressly amends it.

**2. Pricing; Payment.** Prices are as stated in Exhibit A price list,
firm for twelve (12) months. Payment is net forty-five (45) days from
receipt of a correct invoice. Buyer may set off undisputed amounts owed
by Supplier.

**3. Delivery; Risk of Loss.** Delivery terms are FCA (Incoterms 2020)
Supplier's dock, with risk of loss passing on carrier pickup. Time is
of the essence. If delivery is more than ten (10) days late, Buyer may
cancel the affected order without liability.

**4. Inspection; Acceptance.** Buyer may inspect goods within fifteen
(15) business days of receipt and reject nonconforming goods. Rejected
goods will be replaced or refunded at Buyer's option, with return
freight at Supplier's expense.

**5. Warranty.** Supplier warrants that goods will conform to the
specifications and be free from defects in materials and workmanship
for eighteen (18) months from delivery. Supplier will repair, replace,
or refund nonconforming goods. Services will be performed in a
professional and workmanlike manner.

**6. Indemnification.** Supplier will defend and indemnify Buyer
against third-party claims for bodily injury, property damage, or
intellectual-property infringement arising from the goods, except to
the extent caused by Buyer's misuse or modification. Buyer will defend
Supplier against claims arising from Buyer's designs supplied to
Supplier.

**7. Limitation of Liability.** Neither party is liable for indirect,
incidental, or consequential damages. Each party's aggregate liability
is capped at amounts paid or payable under the affected purchase
orders in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim; the cap does not
apply to indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, or
Supplier's warranty obligations.

**8. Term; Termination.** The term is three (3) years. Either party may
terminate for material breach uncured within thirty (30) days of
notice. Buyer may terminate for convenience on sixty (60) days' notice,
paying for conforming goods delivered and non-cancellable work in
progress.

**9. Compliance.** Supplier will comply with applicable laws, including
product-safety and export-control laws, and maintain quality records
for five (5) years.

**10. Confidentiality.** Each party protects the other's non-public
information with reasonable care for three (3) years after disclosure.

**11. Insurance.** Supplier maintains commercial general liability
insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence.

**12. General.** Governed by the laws of the State of Illinois; venue
Cook County. Neither party may assign without consent except to a
merger successor. Entire agreement; written amendments only.
