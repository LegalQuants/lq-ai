# EQUIPMENT PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Equipment Purchase Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of
November 3, 2026 between **Stonebridge Medical Group LLC**, a Colorado
limited liability company ("Buyer"), and **Cobalt Peak Equipment Inc.**,
a Utah corporation ("Supplier").

**1. Sale.** Supplier will sell to Buyer the laboratory refrigeration
units identified in Schedule A (the "Equipment") at the prices stated
therein.

**2. Payment.** One hundred percent (100%) of the purchase price is due
upon order placement, non-refundable.

**3. Delivery; Risk of Loss.** Equipment is delivered EXW (Incoterms
2020) Supplier's warehouse. Risk of loss and title pass to Buyer when
the Equipment is made available at Supplier's dock, whether or not
Buyer has arranged carriage. Buyer bears all freight, insurance, and
import costs. Delivery dates are estimates only, and Supplier has no
liability for delay of any duration.

**4. No Inspection; Deemed Acceptance.** Buyer waives all rights of
inspection. The Equipment is deemed irrevocably accepted upon delivery
to the carrier. Buyer waives any right to revoke acceptance under
applicable law.

**5. WARRANTY DISCLAIMER.** THE EQUIPMENT IS SOLD "AS IS, WHERE IS,
WITH ALL FAULTS." SUPPLIER DISCLAIMS ALL WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR
IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR
A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, OR NON-INFRINGEMENT, AND ANY WARRANTY ARISING
FROM COURSE OF DEALING OR TRADE USAGE.

**6. Exclusive Remedy.** Buyer's sole and exclusive remedy for any
defect discovered within thirty (30) days of delivery is repair by
Supplier at Supplier's then-current service rates, with parts and
shipping at Buyer's expense. No replacement, refund, or cover remedy is
available.

**7. Limitation of Liability.** SUPPLIER'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER
THIS AGREEMENT IS CAPPED AT TEN PERCENT (10%) OF THE PURCHASE PRICE OF
THE UNIT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM. SUPPLIER IS NOT LIABLE FOR SPOILAGE
OF STORED MATERIALS, LOST SAMPLES, OR ANY INDIRECT OR CONSEQUENTIAL
DAMAGES, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THEIR POSSIBILITY.

**8. Indemnification by Buyer.** Buyer shall indemnify and hold
harmless Supplier from all claims arising from Buyer's use, storage, or
resale of the Equipment, including claims alleging design defects.

**9. Termination.** Supplier may cancel any order prior to shipment for
any reason, refunding amounts paid for the cancelled units as Buyer's
sole remedy. Buyer may not cancel any order.

**10. Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the
State of Utah; exclusive venue lies in Salt Lake County, Utah. The
United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of
Goods does not apply.

**11. Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, with Schedule A, is the
entire agreement and supersedes all prior discussions. Amendments must
be in writing signed by both parties.
