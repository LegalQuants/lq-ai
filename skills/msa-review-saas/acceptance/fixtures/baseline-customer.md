# MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as
of June 1, 2026 between **Pinwheel Robotics Inc.**, a Delaware
corporation ("Provider"), and **Stonebridge Medical Group LLC**, a
Colorado limited liability company ("Customer").

**1. The Service.** Provider will make its cloud-based workforce
scheduling platform (the "Service") available to Customer per this
Agreement and any mutually executed order forms. Provider will provide
the Service materially in accordance with its documentation.

**2. Subscription and Fees.** Customer will pay the subscription fees
stated in each order form annually in advance, net thirty (30) days
from invoice. Fees are exclusive of taxes. Provider may increase fees
at renewal by no more than seven percent (7%) with sixty (60) days'
notice.

**3. Term; Termination.** The initial term is one (1) year, renewing
automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party gives
notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before renewal. Either
party may terminate for material breach uncured within thirty (30) days
of written notice. Customer may not terminate for convenience.

**4. Customer Data.** Customer retains all right, title, and interest
in data submitted to the Service ("Customer Data"). Provider will use
Customer Data solely to provide and support the Service and will not
disclose it except as required to provide the Service or as required by
law. Upon termination, Provider will make Customer Data available for
export in CSV format for thirty (30) days.

**5. Security.** Provider maintains an information security program
with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to
protect Customer Data, including encryption in transit and at rest and
annual third-party penetration testing. Provider will notify Customer
without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, of
a confirmed breach of security affecting Customer Data.

**6. Service Levels.** Provider targets 99.5% monthly uptime. If uptime
falls below target, Customer's sole remedy is a service credit of 5% of
the monthly fee per full percentage point below target, capped at 20% of
the monthly fee, claimed within thirty (30) days.

**7. Warranties; Disclaimer.** Each party warrants it has the authority
to enter into this Agreement. Provider warrants the Service will perform
materially per the documentation. EXCEPT AS STATED, THE SERVICE IS
PROVIDED "AS IS" AND PROVIDER DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR
IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

**8. Indemnification.** Provider will defend Customer against
third-party claims that the Service infringes a U.S. patent or
copyright and pay resulting damages finally awarded, provided Customer
promptly notifies Provider and grants sole control of the defense.
Customer will defend Provider against claims arising from Customer Data
or Customer's unlawful use of the Service.

**9. Limitation of Liability.** NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT,
INCIDENTAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES. EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY
IS CAPPED AT THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12)
MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM. THE CAP DOES NOT APPLY TO CUSTOMER'S PAYMENT
OBLIGATIONS OR EITHER PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS.

**10. Confidentiality.** Each party will protect the other's non-public
information with reasonable care, use it only under this Agreement, and
not disclose it except to employees and advisors under confidentiality
obligations. Obligations survive three (3) years after termination.

**11. Suspension.** Provider may suspend the Service on forty-eight
(48) hours' notice for non-payment or a material security risk, limited
in scope and duration to what is reasonably necessary.

**12. General.** This Agreement is governed by Delaware law; exclusive
venue is Wilmington, Delaware. Neither party may assign without consent
except to a successor in a merger or asset sale. This Agreement is the
entire agreement and may be amended only in writing.
