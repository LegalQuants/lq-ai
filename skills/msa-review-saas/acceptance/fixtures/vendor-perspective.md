# CLOUD SERVICES SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Cloud Services Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as
of August 12, 2026 between **Larkspur Financial Services Inc.**, a
Delaware corporation ("Customer"), and **Pinwheel Robotics Inc.**, a
Delaware corporation ("Supplier").

**1. Services.** Supplier will provide the hosted services described in
each order form (the "Services") and all updates, enhancements, and
successor products at no additional charge.

**2. Fees.** Fees are fixed for five (5) years from the Effective Date.
Customer pays quarterly in arrears, net ninety (90) days. Customer may
withhold any invoiced amount it disputes in good faith without limit.

**3. Term; Termination.** The term is five (5) years. Customer may
terminate this Agreement or any order form at any time for convenience
on ten (10) days' notice and receive a refund of all fees paid for the
then-current annual period. Supplier may terminate only for Customer's
material breach uncured within ninety (90) days.

**4. Service Levels.** Supplier guarantees 99.99% monthly uptime. For
each 0.1% below target, Customer receives a credit of 10% of the
monthly fee; if uptime falls below 99.5% in any two months of a
quarter, Customer may terminate for cause and recover all fees paid in
the preceding twelve (12) months.

**5. Customer Data.** Customer owns all Customer Data and all analytics,
usage data, and derived data generated by the Services. Supplier will
return all such data in any format Customer requests within five (5)
days of any request, at no charge.

**6. Warranties.** Supplier warrants the Services will be uninterrupted
and error-free and will conform to all documentation, marketing
materials, and proposals provided to Customer.

**7. Indemnification.** Supplier shall indemnify, defend, and hold
harmless Customer and its affiliates from and against any and all
claims, losses, and expenses arising out of or relating to the
Services, including claims arising from Customer's use of the Services,
without limitation.

**8. Limitation of Liability.** SUPPLIER'S LIABILITY UNDER THIS
AGREEMENT IS UNLIMITED. CUSTOMER'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY IS CAPPED AT THE
FEES PAID IN THE ONE (1) MONTH PRECEDING THE CLAIM AND EXCLUDES ALL
INDIRECT DAMAGES.

**9. Audit.** Customer may audit Supplier's facilities, systems, and
records relating to the Services on twenty-four (24) hours' notice, no
more than four (4) times per year, at Supplier's expense.

**10. Insurance.** Supplier will maintain cyber-liability insurance of
not less than $20,000,000 per occurrence and name Customer as an
additional insured.

**11. Confidentiality.** Each party protects the other's confidential
information with reasonable care. Customer's obligations expire one (1)
year after disclosure; Supplier's obligations are perpetual.

**12. General.** Governed by New York law; venue New York County.
Customer may assign freely; Supplier may not assign without consent.
Entire agreement; amendments in writing.
