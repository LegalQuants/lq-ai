# FOXGLOVE MARKETING LLC — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective date: February 1, 2026**

Foxglove Marketing LLC ("Foxglove", "we") provides marketing-analytics
services to business customers. This policy explains how we collect,
use, share, and protect personal information.

## Information we collect

We collect: (a) account information you provide (name, work email,
company, role); (b) customer content our clients upload to the service
(contact records, campaign data); (c) usage data collected
automatically (log data, device type, IP address, pages viewed); and
(d) support communications.

## How we use information

We use personal information to provide and secure the service, process
transactions, provide support, send service communications, improve the
service using aggregated and de-identified data, and comply with law.
We do not sell personal information, and we do not use customer content
to train machine-learning models.

## How we share information

We share personal information with: service providers under written
contracts limiting their use to providing services to us (hosting,
payment processing, support tooling); our affiliates under this policy;
parties to a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy; and
authorities when required by law, with notice to affected customers
where legally permitted. A current list of subprocessors is available
at our trust page and by request.

## International transfers

We are U.S.-based and host data in the United States and Ireland. Where
we transfer personal data from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we rely on
the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent
UK and Swiss mechanisms.

## Retention

We retain account information for the life of the account plus twelve
(12) months. Customer content is retained for the subscription term and
deleted within sixty (60) days of termination. Log data is retained for
thirteen (13) months.

## Your rights and choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct,
delete, port, or restrict processing of your personal information, and
to object to processing. Submit requests to privacy@foxglove.example or
via our web form; we respond within thirty (30) days (forty-five (45)
days for CCPA requests, extendable as permitted). You may appeal a
refusal by replying to our decision. We do not discriminate against
rights requests. Where we process customer content on behalf of a
client, we will refer your request to that client.

## Security

We maintain a written security program including encryption in transit
and at rest, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication
for personnel, annual third-party penetration testing, and an
incident-response plan. We notify affected customers of a personal-data
breach without undue delay consistent with applicable law.

## Children

The service is for business use. We do not knowingly collect
information from children under 16, and we delete such information if
discovered.

## Contact and complaints

Questions or complaints: privacy@foxglove.example, or Foxglove
Marketing LLC, Attn: Privacy, 12 Illustrative Lane, New York, NY. EEA
and UK individuals may contact our EU representative at
eurep@foxglove.example and may lodge a complaint with their supervisory
authority.

## Changes to this policy

We will post changes here and, for material changes, notify account
owners by email at least thirty (30) days before they take effect.
