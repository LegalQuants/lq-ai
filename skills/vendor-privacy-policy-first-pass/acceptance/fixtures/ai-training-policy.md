# JUNIPER GRID SYSTEMS — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective date: April 10, 2026**

This policy describes how Juniper Grid Systems Inc. ("Juniper", "we")
handles personal information in connection with our transcription
products.

## Information we collect

Account details (name, email, organization); content you submit,
including meeting audio, video, and transcripts ("Customer Content");
usage and device data; and information from integration partners you
connect.

## How we use information

We use information to provide the services, secure our platform, and
develop new features. **We use Customer Content, including meeting
recordings and transcripts, to train and improve our machine-learning
and artificial-intelligence models.** Model-training use is integral to
the service and applies to all accounts.

## Sharing

We share information with service providers; with our advertising and
measurement partners, including hashed contact identifiers and usage
segments, for interest-based advertising; with integration partners at
your direction; and with authorities when required by law.

## International transfers

We process data in the United States and may process it in other
countries where we or our providers operate.

## Retention

We retain Customer Content while your account is active. Content
incorporated into model training may be retained in derived form
indefinitely.

## Your rights

You may access or delete your account information from settings.
Requests regarding Customer Content should be directed to the account
owner. California and EEA residents may have additional rights under
applicable law; contact privacy@junipergrid.example.

## Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit,
and we test our systems regularly. We will notify affected accounts of
security incidents as required by applicable law.

## Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13.

## Contact

Juniper Grid Systems Inc., 15 Illustrative Parkway, Reno, Nevada.
privacy@junipergrid.example.
