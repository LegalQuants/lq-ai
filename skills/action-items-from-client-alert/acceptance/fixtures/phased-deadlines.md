# CLIENT ALERT — MERIDIAN COUNSEL GROUP

**August 3, 2026**

## Fictional "Accessibility in Digital Commerce" Rule Finalized: Phased
Deadlines Through 2028

On July 22, 2026, the fictional Federal Digital Commerce Commission
published the final Accessibility in Digital Commerce Rule (the
"Rule"). The Rule takes **effect on September 1, 2026**, but its
substantive obligations phase in by company size over two years.
Compliance dates differ from the effective date — calendar both.

### Phase 1 — Large platforms (annual revenue over $500 million)

Covered large platforms must bring their consumer-facing web
interfaces into conformance with the Rule's accessibility standard by
**July 1, 2027** (Rule §4). By the same date, they must publish an
accessibility statement identifying a designated accessibility contact
(Rule §5).

### Phase 2 — All covered platforms

All covered platforms, regardless of size, must conform their mobile
applications by **July 1, 2028** (Rule §6). Beginning with fiscal years
starting on or after **January 1, 2028**, covered platforms must file
an annual conformance certification with the Commission within 90 days
of fiscal year end (Rule §7) — a recurring obligation.

### Enforcement discretion

The Commission announced it will exercise enforcement discretion for
Phase 1 obligations until **October 1, 2027**, for platforms that can
demonstrate a documented remediation plan adopted before July 1, 2027
(Rule §9). The discretion window does not change the compliance dates
themselves.

### Practical notes

Covered companies should determine which phase applies, budget
remediation work into 2026-2027 planning cycles, and stand up the
certification workflow required by §7 before the first covered fiscal
year begins.
