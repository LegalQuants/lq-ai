## Fictional Appellate Decision Clarifies "Consequential Damages" Waivers
in Commercial Contracts

### The decision

The court held that a standard mutual waiver of consequential damages
did not bar recovery of liquidated damages expressly agreed in the same
contract, reasoning that liquidated damages are a bargained-for direct
remedy rather than a species of consequential loss. The court declined
to adopt the narrower rule urged by the supplier, under which any
damages measured by lost profits would be treated as consequential
regardless of contractual labeling.

### Why it matters

The decision aligns New Cambria with the majority approach in other
fictional jurisdictions and reduces uncertainty about whether
liquidated-damages schedules survive broad waiver boilerplate. Parties
drafting supply agreements governed by New Cambria law can expect
liquidated-damages provisions to be enforced according to their terms
even alongside consequential-damages waivers, provided the provision is
a reasonable pre-estimate of loss.
