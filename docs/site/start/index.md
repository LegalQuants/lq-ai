Five pages, roughly in the order most readers want them. None is a prerequisite for another except the quickstart's own install steps — skip anything that doesn't match what you're trying to do, or jump straight to [choose your path](choose-your-path.md) to route by goal instead of reading in order.

- **[Is LQ.AI for you?](../../start/is-it-for-you.md)** — checkable disqualifiers, before you invest more time. For an evaluator deciding whether to spend an afternoon on a deployment, or an operator fielding "why not buy something instead" from a colleague. After it, you can name the specific reason LQ.AI is or isn't a fit for your team today, rather than guessing from marketing copy.
- **[What it touches](../../start/what-it-touches.md)** — prerequisites, ports, and where data actually lives, before you run a command. For a technical evaluator or operator who wants the host requirements, the port map, and the data-boundary shape before the first `docker compose up`, not after something collides or a security review asks. After it, you know what has to already be true on the host and which single row of data ever leaves your environment.
- **[Quickstart](../../quickstart.md)** — clone to a citation-grounded skill result in about 20 minutes. For an operator or evaluator ready to try it, with recovery steps placed at the point a first run commonly stalls. After it, you have a running deployment, an Organization Profile, a Project, and a verified citation result you can read against the source document.
- **[Choose your path](choose-your-path.md)** — route straight to the namespace that matches your goal. For anyone who already knows what they're trying to do and doesn't want to read the other four pages first. After it, you're on the page for your actual goal, with explicit permission to skip the rest.
- **[Where help lives](../../start/where-help-lives.md)** — day-to-day product help is in the running application, not on this site; the one exception. For anyone searching this site for a how-do-I-use-this-feature answer. After it, you know to check the in-app Learn tab first, and which incident procedures live here instead because they carry professional-conduct stakes.

Once you're past this namespace, [Operate](../operate/index.md) covers installing on a specific topology and running a deployment day to day, and [Trust](../trust/index.md) is where the evidence for a security or procurement review lives.
