LQ.AI is a self-hosted, open-source AI platform for in-house legal teams: it runs on infrastructure you control, against model-provider keys you supply, and every artifact that shapes an answer — the skills, the playbooks, the citation-verification logic — ships as source you can read, fork, and audit rather than a vendor's hidden prompt.

## Where your data goes

The Inference Gateway is the only component that calls out to a model provider; the backend never holds a provider key and never makes an external call of its own. Every request is classified onto one of five Inference Tiers, from fully air-gapped local inference (Tier 1), through customer-hosted cloud inference (Tier 2) and enterprise managed inference with ZDR / no-training commitments (Tier 3), through a standard cloud API (Tier 4) to a consumer or free-tier endpoint (Tier 5), and the routed tier is written to the audit log and shown on the message that used it (README.md, "Architecture"; docs/architecture.md, "Where data lives, in detail"). [The trust centre](trust/index.md) has the full accounting, artifact by artifact, with what each one covers and what stays yours to verify.

In-house work is the focus, not a gate on who you are: the PRD's primary user is "in-house counsel at organizations of any size, from solo General Counsel to enterprise legal departments" (PRD §1.4), and every v1 exclusion is a use case rather than a practitioner type (PRD §1.6). Not sure this is the right tool for your team yet? [Read the disqualifiers](start/is-it-for-you.md) — they are checkable, and they are the place to decide you are out, before you spend more time here.
