The matter

Closed-set ToolIntents

The planner may choose ONLY from PLANNER_ALLOWLIST: retrieve_chunks, retrieve_caselaw, call_mcp_tool, retrieve_authority. guard.py's R6 separately re-checks the choice against the broader PHASE_GRANTS[analysis] (also run_skill, run_playbook, propose_precedent, plan itself) — defense in depth, not a second decision point the model gets to use.

Brakes — flip one off (R5 → R6 → R4, plus the step cap)

Known gaps
